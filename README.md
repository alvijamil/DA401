# DA401 Capstone Project - Jamil Alvi

## Project Title
Bank Lending Standards and Investment-Grade Corporate Credit Spreads

## Research Question
Does the Federal Reserve’s Senior Loan Officer Opinion Survey on Bank Lending Practices (SLOOS) improve one-month-ahead models of Aaa and Baa corporate credit-spread changes after market and macroeconomic indicators are included?

## Data Source
All data are public and available through FRED. The sample will use monthly data from 1997–2025. I will use each SLOOS value only after its Federal Reserve release date.

## Methods
I will estimate separate models for Aaa and Baa spread changes; here are some of the shortlisted models:

- Exploratory time-series analysis
- OLS regression
- LASSO regression
- No-change and prior-change benchmark models
- Expanding-window evaluation
- Mean absolute error and root mean squared error

I will compare models with SLOOS to identical models without SLOOS to determine whether the survey reduces out-of-sample error.
